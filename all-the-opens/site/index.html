The article, enriched

The open knowledge ecosystem is already connected. Almost nothing shows you the connections.

These pages take an English Wikipedia article and render it with the open ecosystem's media and cited sources placed beside the prose — Internet Archive scans, Commons files, OpenLibrary editions, court opinions. Nothing is placed by hand. Every item was found by an identifier the article itself states: a citation's ISBN, OCLC or LCCN, or a wikilink's Wikidata QID. There is no per-article code and, on the three pages below, no curated dataset.

The hard case

An edge with no identifier on either side

Ludwig Prandtl's 1899 dissertation is scanned and public at the Internet Archive. It carries no ISBN, no OCLC, no LCCN. Wikidata knows of it only as Q72419729 — an item with an author, a date and an institution, and no label in any language. Nothing is shared between the two records to join on.

So the match is made on the object Wikidata describes (P1026), and the page shows its work:

All three signals are required, because the same collection holds theses by Ludwig, Hans and Antonius Prandtl. The card is dashed and says corroborated, not identified, because a description that agrees is a weaker claim than an identifier that matches, and it must not be allowed to read like one.

Honesty

What this does not do

  • You can't type an article in. These four pages are pre-generated. Making it live needs streaming and a server, and that decision hasn't been made.
  • Coverage is the real bottleneck, not extraction. Of the unique identifiers cited across these articles, most reach no Internet Archive record at all. The query is right; the answer is genuinely nothing. That is a fact about the ecosystem, not a bug in the pipeline — so each section states how many of its sources you can actually open, and a section of dead ends says so rather than just looking thin.
  • Where an article states no identifier, nothing appears. There is no fuzzy title search filling the gaps, because a guess presented beside a verified match would poison every match on the page.
